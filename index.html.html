Our Story

More than
a café,a feeling.

Nestled in the heart of Kala Ghoda — Mumbai's iconic art precinct — our café has been a gathering place for artists, writers, and coffee lovers since 2004.

We source beans from single-origin farms in Coorg and Chikmagalur, roasted fresh every week. Every cup tells a story. Every bite is crafted with intention.
